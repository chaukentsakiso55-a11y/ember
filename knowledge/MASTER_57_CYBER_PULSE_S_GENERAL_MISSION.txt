57. CYBER PULSE'S GENERAL MISSION
================================================================================

Cyber Pulse can be summarized as a youth technology ecosystem focused on:

- building practical student technology
- experimenting with advanced AI
- improving learning
- improving digital safety
- making AI more capable
- building offline-first systems
- creating connected apps
- exploring future computing
- turning ideas from team members into functional technology

The organization is strongly driven by DARTHWOLF's development work.
