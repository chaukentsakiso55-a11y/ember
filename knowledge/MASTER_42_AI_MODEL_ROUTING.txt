42. AI MODEL ROUTING
================================================================================

Many DARTHWOLF projects use the idea of multiple AI providers instead of relying on only one.

Model-routing concepts:
- local model first
- cloud model when needed
- different model for coding
- different model for reasoning
- fallback providers
- OpenRouter support
- model benchmarking
- automatic route selection

This appears in:
- Infinity OS
- Star AI
- Pulsar AI
- Study AI
- AEGIS
