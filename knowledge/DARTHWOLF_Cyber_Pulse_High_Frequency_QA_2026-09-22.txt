WHAT IS STUDYLOCK?
A Student/Parent/Teacher focus-and-learning ecosystem combining protected study sessions, app blocking, tutoring, offline learning, quizzes, notes, planning, progress, pairing/sync, family controls and teacher content. Its current visual family uses liquid yellow/orange.

WHAT IS STUDYOS?

WHAT IS STUDY AI?
A dedicated education AI assistant for tutoring and learning assistance, with online/offline model direction, Firebase integration and Android APK development. It is not the same as StudyLock.

WHAT IS STAR AI?
A general-purpose assistant for work, learning, research, productivity, coding and everyday tasks. Its direction includes Android/desktop, online and offline models, multiple providers, voice, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, background operation and memory.
