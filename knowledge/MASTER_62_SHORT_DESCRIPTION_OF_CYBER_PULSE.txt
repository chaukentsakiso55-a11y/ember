62. SHORT DESCRIPTION OF CYBER PULSE
================================================================================

Cyber Pulse is a youth-built technology organization founded and led by DARTHWOLF / Ntsakiso Chauke. It focuses on building AI-powered education tools, digital safety systems, intelligent assistants, automation platforms, mobile applications, future-computing concepts, and connected technologies. The organization brings together ideas from multiple contributors, while DARTHWOLF performs much of the development, integration, system design, testing, and expansion needed to turn those ideas into working products.
